RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Sustainability begins with thoughtful planning.

The DPR outlines preliminary plans for water treatment, recycling and reuse, effluent treatment, utility systems and environmental safeguards as part of the proposed manufacturing complex.

01

Water stewardship

Proposed raw-water storage, filtration, reverse osmosis, ultra-pure water systems, effluent treatment and water recycling.

02

Responsible utilities

Planning for industrial utilities, controlled manufacturing environments, fire protection and segregated chemical storage.

03

Technology development

The DPR identifies TOPCon and future TOPCon–Perovskite tandem technology as part of its longer-term technology roadmap.

PEOPLE & OPPORTUNITY

Creating pathways for skills and opportunity.

The DPR estimates 649 direct jobs and approximately 471 indirect jobs across the proposed project. These are planning estimates, not current vacancies or confirmed employment.
