COMPANY OVERVIEW

A connected approach to renewable energy manufacturing.

Yedlapalli Greenenergy Private Limited's DPR outlines a greenfield project in Andhra Pradesh covering mono-crystalline silicon wafers, N-type TOPCon solar cells, solar module assembly, lithium-ion battery module and pack assembly, and a battery energy storage system.

Project status: The DPR describes a proposed greenfield project. This website therefore uses planning language such as “proposed”, “planned” and “target” until the company confirms the latest implementation status.
SOLAR MANUFACTURING

Three stages. One integrated vision.

01 / WAFERS

Mono-crystalline silicon wafers

The DPR proposes large-format N-type wafer production, supported by ingot growth, cropping, wire sawing, cleaning, sorting and inspection systems.

1.2 GWOutline capacity per annum
Page 2 of DPR; page 5 describes 1.0 GW annual wafer output considering approximately 20% loss. Confirm final public figure.
02 / SOLAR CELLS

N-type TOPCon cells

The proposed process includes wafer texturing, diffusion, tunnel oxide and polysilicon deposition, passivation, metallization, firing, testing and sorting.

25–25.5%Stated efficiency target
Proposed annual capacity: 1,200 MW. DPR page 6.
03 / MODULES

Bifacial solar modules

The module assembly plan includes cell stringing, glass preparation, lay-up, lamination, framing, junction box installation and final testing.

1.0 GWProposed annual assembly
Bifacial modules are identified as the principal product. DPR pages 2 and 7.
